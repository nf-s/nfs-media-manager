# Browsing and navigation

## Requirements

- BROWSE-01: Albums and films each support table, compact list and image-first grid. No hero banners, explanatory paragraphs, gradient surfaces or nested bordered cards. Text is high contrast; dividers are subtle. Tags may be pills.
- BROWSE-02: Keep library navigation, universal search, sorting, sort direction, view toggle and result count close together. One Filters button opens a drawer from the right with collapsible Views, Smart playlists (albums), Ranges, Listening (albums) and Columns (table) sections. Active filter chips stay in the navigation rail or mobile filter row.
- BROWSE-03: Grid and table reserve the full matching result height as soon as the total is known. Scrollbar jumps load only the viewport/overscan pages, never every intervening page. Keep the first page for the total and only the active window in cache; cancel obsolete requests and discard their results. Reset on any query change, including same-count results. Use stable ordering with an ID tie-breaker. Show a retry action for failed windows without collapsing their reserved space.
- BROWSE-09: Unloaded slots retain their exact row/card geometry with a subtle wipe skeleton, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. DOM and page cache must remain bounded during long sessions. Scrolling must stay inside the fixed shell; keyboard scrolling, responsive grid columns, column resizing and detail return position remain usable.
- BROWSE-04: Table headings remain visible; rows are compact and columns independently selectable, resizable and saved per library. Horizontal scrolling must not widen the whole page. Long values truncate without losing access to their full content.
- BROWSE-05: Grid cards use square album art or portrait film posters and contain artwork, title, artist/director and concise release/sort information. Ratings sit in a strip over the artwork; the active sort value and tag pills can be shown over the artwork too, each switchable under Filters > Views > On artwork and saved per library. Sorting by a card rating highlights that rating instead. Missing or failed art has a visible fallback and never removes a record. External links remain available without opening details.
- BROWSE-06: Selecting an item opens an accessible detail panel: a drawer over the results from the right, or a bottom sheet on phones. While it is open, Up/Down in the results moves the selection. The sorted field is pinned first among the detail metrics. Close, Escape and browser Back preserve query, loaded rows and scroll. A direct detail URL remains usable. Keyboard focus returns to the trigger.
- BROWSE-07: Persist view, columns, sorting and filters independently for albums and films; URL state wins over stored defaults. Mobile controls stay within two rows, cards shrink to at least two columns (three at standard density on a phone), and touch actions do not depend on hover.
- BROWSE-08: Loading, errors, empty results and completed pagination are explicit. No blank infinite spinner. Show filtered total, not just currently loaded count.
- BROWSE-10: Grid density is configurable and saved independently per library. Compact mobile cards prioritize artwork and legible title/artist information, with touch-accessible details and outbound links. Changing density recomputes virtual row geometry without unbounded loading, overlap or horizontal page overflow. Album titles linked to Spotify must not be accompanied by a duplicate Spotify action in the same card or detail heading.

- BROWSE-11: One Library selector (Albums, Tracks, Films, Subscriptions) scopes Browse, Tags, Imports, Jobs and Providers; Subscriptions has Browse, Channels, Imports, Jobs and Providers and no Tags. Switching libraries keeps the section where applicable, not stale previews, selections or item links. The activity control shows every library and links to the correct scoped jobs or imports. See [workspace navigation](../import-navigation.md).
- BROWSE-12: Subscriptions (`/subscriptions`) browses YouTube videos and podcast episodes together with grid and list views, following BROWSE-02, 03, 04, 06, 08 and 09. The list uses the shared compact table; cards use 16:9 thumbnails with the type and length on the image. Persist view, columns, sort and filters as in BROWSE-07 under the Subscriptions library. An empty library links to Imports. See [Subscriptions](../subscriptions.md#browse-page).

## Acceptance

Jump directly from the first page to near the end in grid and table: verify only nearby pages are requested, the scrollbar height is unchanged, and the last partial row has no invented items. Scroll rapidly and change filters while requests are pending; stale results must never enter the new view. Verify bounded DOM/cache, zero results, retry after an error, reduced motion, and opening/closing an item without losing position. Test 390px and desktop widths, keyboard-only navigation, column resize, rating links and failed artwork.
