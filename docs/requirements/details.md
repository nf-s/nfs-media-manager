# Details, edits and history

## Requirements

- DETAIL-01: Every item opens with the same header: artwork, kind, title, creator or parent links, one line of facts, the item's service logos (in one muted grey, each opening the service in a new tab) on a line of their own, then the item's actions with More at the end. Where the item can be played, Play and Queue sit under the artwork and fill its width: Play labelled, Queue a square icon button. Settings such as a playlist's type are a ticked group in More, not controls in the header. On a phone More moves to the drawer bar and a header with no other action has no action row. Overview comes first and holds the item's contents: tracks for an album or playlist, films for a person, albums or films for a collection, videos or episodes for a channel. Tabs, in order: album Overview, Listening, Sources, Edit; track Overview, Sources; artist Overview, Albums, Tracks, Listening, Coverage, Sources; playlist Overview, Listening, Coverage, Sources; film Overview, Sources, Edit; person, collection, channel, subscription item and tag Overview, Sources. A tab exists only when it holds more than one block: an album's tracks and playlists and a track's playlists and listening chart are sections of Overview. Metadata coverage is never in Overview; an artist or playlist shows it on its Coverage tab. Overview leads with at most four headline figures, then the contents, tags (one section per group, clicking filters the library) and at most six facts; an album or film's **All fields** opens every stored field with the providers that supply it. An album, track, artist or playlist leads its figures with scrobbles, with the all-time sparkline beside the count, and last played; an artist and a playlist read both from the listening series, so the two are empty until it loads. Tag groups, Playlists and Details are sections of the same form, set flush left, and a section's one action is a link button at the end of its heading (**Manage tags** on the first tag group, **Add to playlist**, **All fields**). A detail reached from a filtered library shows **Browse with N filters**, a small button that returns to that library with the filters applied. Every tab can be linked to; older tab values (`tracks`, `playlists`, `listening` on a track, `sync`, `films`, `videos`, `episodes`, `history`) open the tab that now holds their content. The standalone page uses the same header and tabs; above 1000px its Overview puts reference sections (tags, playlists, details) in a side column. Provider statistics and where a tag came from are answered in Sources. Sources lists one row per provider: its match state, identifier, time since the last fetch and its queue state when it is waiting, paused or needs attention. A row opens in place to show its identifier form, where the identifier came from, what the provider supplies, stored provider information, and one history list of direct responses, snapshots and jobs, newest first. Raw snapshots open from the history on demand.
- DETAIL-02: Open an item in one inspector over the current view. Internal detail links replace the visible item in the inspector and add to its history. Back, browser history, reload and copied links restore the selected detail and its view settings. The inspector's bar shows the path as breadcrumbs: the library, each item opened on the way, then the current item, and each step returns to it; a job in a batch shows its batch as a step. On phones the bar shows Back, the title, More and Close. **Open page** opens a standalone detail. Every header ends with a **More** menu: the item's less used actions (for example Edit, Merge or a filter of its library), then **Open page** in the inspector and **Copy link**. Clicking filterable metadata applies a filter to the relevant library. Closing restores focus and scroll.
- DETAIL-03: Canonical title, art, source and supported album/film fields can be overridden without overwriting provider snapshots. Show saved/error states and refresh list/detail after saving.
- DETAIL-04: Provider identifier correction belongs in Sources, separate from canonical field overrides in Edit. Re-scrape uses the corrected identifier. Record actor/time/reason; allow clearing an override to reveal the underlying value. The identifier is edited in its source row. Saving an album or film identifier also queues a refresh from that provider when the provider can refresh now (the button reads **Save and refresh**; otherwise **Save**). Artist identifier corrections are applied by **Save and refresh** and retained on success.
- DETAIL-05: History lists timestamp, provider, provenance, status and payload inspection. Every distinct imported/scraped payload is retained. Reimporting an identical source payload is idempotent; do not erase old history. In Sources the history of a provider merges its direct provider responses, stored snapshots and jobs into one list, with **Show older**. A direct response opens the stored request details and response. A metadata snapshot opens its normalized data and stored payload. Entries originating from a job link to that job.
- DETAIL-06: **Refresh all** in the Sources summary line queues a refresh for every eligible provider; each row has its own Refresh. Show job/progress feedback with a link to each queued job. Respect active jobs, cooldowns and unavailable providers. No action fires on mount, on opening a row or its history, or on saving a filter. Read-only source rows do not imply scraping or snapshots that the record does not support. A provider without a usable identifier shows **Needs ID** and one action: **Find match** where the provider can be searched (DETAIL-11), otherwise **Add ID**, which opens the identifier form.
- DETAIL-07: Filterable detail values use the same compact pill treatment, including source and watched status. Artist, director, actor, channel and collection names open their own details; filtering the library is an explicit action (for example **Filter films as director**, **Show in Albums**). Albums and playlists inside another item's rows open their detail; only their own detail, card or the row of a library list plays them. Active filter pills share Any / Required / Excluded colors and behavior with browse filters. Shift-click keeps the page/inspector open for adding multiple filters. Do not repeat funnel icons on individual values in details, tables or grids.
- DETAIL-08: Clicking a tag label filters. Clicking a stored collection label opens its collection details in the shared inspector, pushes panel history and preserves the album or film underneath. Use the canonical collection membership ID; never fetch the collection directory or infer an ID from its name. A name without a stored membership ID remains plain text. Use **Filter albums** or **Filter films** to open the corresponding library with that collection selected. Tag and collection pills carry no source icons: a collection's header and Sources tab link to the original collection when stored evidence identifies it, and a tag's aliases and paginated provenance, distinguishing item origins from those merged elsewhere, are in the tag drawer's Sources. Opening sources reads stored evidence only. Escape closes the nested surface and restores focus without closing media details. Never guess upstream IDs.
- DETAIL-09: Jobs share one table across the Jobs page and provider pages. Selecting a job name or following an individual job link opens the shared details inspector with Overview, Request and response, and Logs tabs. Overview leads with the job summary (headline, error code, retry time) and its actions in the order the server gives them: Retry, Find match (the provider's row in the item's Sources, with the search open), Open provider, Resume provider, Sign in and Add key (the provider page). Request and response lists each saved snapshot and diagnostic collapsed, each with Copy. Preserve browser history, parent detail tabs, table filters and scroll position; checkboxes remain bulk selection. Legacy job links and the standalone job page (`/jobs/<id>`, with breadcrumbs to Jobs and its batch) remain available. Viewing details only reads saved data.
- DETAIL-10: Field provenance (`GET /api/v1/media/:id/provenance`) lists each stored album or film field (columns, declared browse values and credits) with its stored value, its latest manual override and every provider whose current normalized result supplies it, newest first, with the snapshot, its date and job and whether that value matches the stored one. Several providers can supply one field and the app does not record which write was applied, so no single provider is claimed. It is derived at read time from current provider state and overrides, and returns no payloads or credentials. Tags keep their own provenance in the tag drawer. **All fields** (in an overview's Details) shows this as one table: field, stored value, the providers whose value matches the stored one (marks, with the scrape date in the tooltip), a count of providers that supply a different value, **Edited** for a manual override, and when the value last changed (the override, else the newest matching scrape). Values of any type are shown: dates as ISO dates, numbers grouped, names as pills, other values as compact JSON, and an en dash when nothing is stored.
- DETAIL-11: **Find match** searches one provider for an album, film or artist from its source row and lists up to 20 candidates with artwork, creator, year and a link to the provider. It opens with the item's own title and creator and the text can be changed. **Choose** stores the candidate's identifier exactly as **Save and refresh** would; the stored one is marked **Current**. A row whose latest lookup found several records offers **Choose** and lists the candidates that lookup kept, without a new request. The search (`POST /api/v1/find-match/search`) is one request through the provider's request policy, so a provider that is halted, cooling down or over budget refuses it with the reason, and a provider without stored credentials is not called. Typed text is sent as plain text: MusicBrainz query syntax is escaped. Candidate links and artwork must be HTTP(S) URLs. `GET /api/v1/find-match/unresolved` reads stored jobs only and answers 404 for an item that does not exist. Spotify, MusicBrainz, Last.fm and Discogs search albums, TMDb searches films, and Spotify, Last.fm, Discogs and MusicBrainz search artists. Other providers keep **Add ID**. An opened row always holds the identifier form as well.
- DETAIL-12: When a user requests a result they expect to see immediately, use a direct request. Serve usable saved data first. Use a job for work that needs background execution or continuation, such as a scheduled sync, bulk enrichment or an explicit full refresh. Show an accessible loading state as soon as the user acts, keep it visible until the result or error appears, and identify the provider while waiting on it. Give the reason for failure or throttling and a way to retry. Apply the same provider limits, capture policy and redaction on both paths. Save each eligible response, including partial and unsuccessful responses, before reporting success. In Sources, link the response to the item and the resulting relationships. For work performed by a job, also link to that job.
- TAG-01: Counts, aliases, merges, provenance and visibility belong to one library. A tag opens in the shared detail drawer like every other item (Overview: merged names, hide, unhide and merge; Sources: detailed provenance), joins the drawer's Back history, and its page link opens the Tags page with the tag selected. Hide removes a group from display and autocomplete, not stored membership or existing explicit filters. Reimports preserve visibility; a merge involving a hidden group stays hidden until explicitly unhidden. Manage hidden groups separately, with loaded-row and Shift-range selection. See [tag and collection sources](../tag-source-navigation.md).
- TAG-02: A tag can have several parent tags within its library. A filter on a tag matches that tag and every visible tag beneath it, and suggestion counts cover the same items and state the number of subtags. Tags → Hierarchy shows the tree with each tag's own and combined item counts; parents are edited from a tag's details and from a selection in Tags → All. A tag cannot sit beneath itself. Hidden subtags are left out of a parent, and a merge carries parents and subtags to the kept name. See [tag hierarchy](../tag-hierarchy.md).

## Acceptance

Open, edit, inspect history, return to the same scroll location, then deep-link and reload. Test errors and empty history. Mock job execution for corrected identifiers. Verify important content is visible without expanding JSON and raw credentials never appear in history or browser responses.
